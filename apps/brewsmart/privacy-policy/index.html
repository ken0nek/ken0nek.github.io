BrewSmart

Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 18, 2026

BrewSmart helps you dial in, time, and log coffee brews. It has no accounts, and your library works without a connection. It is free, with no ads and no subscription; optional tips are processed by Apple, so we do not receive your payment details.

Your library stays on your device. Features you use, such as sharing by link and reading a web page for a recipe, send what they need off it. The app also uses the internet for things such as usage analytics (see Analytics), tips through the App Store, and checking whether this version is still supported, a request that carries no identifier.

On your device

Recipes, beans, brew logs, notes, and preferences are stored on your device. They leave it when you use a feature that sends them, such as sharing by link. Syncing between your iPhone and Apple Watch does not pass through our servers.

Analytics

BrewSmart and brewsmart.app use third-party analytics services to understand how they are used and to improve them. They receive usage information, such as which features are used, broad details of a brew such as its method and rating, whether actions succeed, and tips, together with a random identifier for your device or installation. They may also record your country. We do not send them your name, email address or Apple ID, the text of your recipes or notes, or the text of any web page, and when someone opens a share link in a browser, the shared recipe or bean is not sent to them. These services may process this information in countries other than your own.

Sharing by link

Sharing by link may store a copy of that recipe or bean on our servers so the recipient can open it, or may put the recipe or bean in the link itself. The stored copy carries no account or device identifier.

Anyone with the link can open it, so treat it as public. Stored copies do not expire on their own; contact us to remove one.

Reading a web page

This happens when you ask for it, by giving the app a link or sending it a page from another app.

BrewSmart sends what it needs to read the page, such as its text and address, to our server, which passes it to AI providers to extract the recipe. You review the result before anything is saved. In some cases, such as when a page you send from another app takes too long to read, BrewSmart finishes reading it later and saves the recipe without showing it to you first. You can then edit or delete it.

We send whatever the page shows you, so avoid pages behind a login or showing information meant only for you. Reading web pages has a usage limit, counted against an identifier based on information Apple provides about your download of the app. It is the same on each device that uses your Apple Account, and it stays the same after you reinstall. It does not contain your name or your Apple ID. If that information is not available, the app uses a random identifier kept on your device instead. Our server keeps a usage count under it for a limited time. Request data, such as the page text, address, prompts, and response, goes to observability providers for debugging and quality, retained under their terms.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No access to your device's location.
  • No advertising identifiers and no cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not train our own models on your recipes, notes, or the pages you read into the app.

Retention

Your library stays on your device until you delete it or the app. Stored share links persist until removed on request. Usage counts for that limit are kept for a limited time. Analytics services follow their own retention. Request traces and server logs follow each provider's retention.

Third parties

We use providers such as hosting providers (website, API, share-link storage), AI providers (recipe extraction), observability providers (request traces), analytics services, content delivery networks (scripts such as the one behind recipe embeds on brewsmart.app), and Apple (distribution, tips, device sync — see Apple's privacy policy).

We describe them by role rather than by name so this stays accurate when providers change. What each receives is described above; as with any web request, a provider your device contacts also sees its IP address.

Your rights and contact

There are no accounts. Deleting the app removes your library from your device; contact us to remove a stored share link. If you live under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws, contact us to exercise your rights. BrewSmart is a general-audience app and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Material changes update the date above. Questions: brewsmart@ken0nek.com.
