BrewSmart

Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 4, 2026

BrewSmart helps you dial in, time, and log coffee brews. It has no accounts, works offline, and collects as little as possible. It is free, with no ads and no subscription; the optional tip jar is processed by Apple, so we never see your payment details.

Your library stays on your device. Two features send data off it, and only when you use them: sharing by link, and reading a web page for a recipe.

On your device

Recipes, beans, brew logs, notes, and preferences are stored on your device and are not sent to us. Syncing between your iPhone and Apple Watch uses Apple's own frameworks and does not pass through our servers.

Analytics

BrewSmart and brewsmart.app send anonymous, aggregate signals — which features are used, whether an action succeeded — to one or more privacy-focused analytics providers. They are tied only to a random per-install identifier, never to your name, email, or Apple ID, and never include your recipes, notes, or the text of any web page. Providers may process them outside your country, including in the EU and the US.

Sharing by link

Creating a share link stores that recipe or bean on our servers so the recipient can open it. The stored copy carries no account or device identifier and nothing that connects it to you.

Anyone with the link can open it, so treat it as public. Links do not expire on their own; contact us to remove one.

Reading a web page

This happens only when you ask for it, by giving the app a link or sending it a page from another app. BrewSmart never browses or crawls on its own.

The app reduces the page to plain text on your device, then sends that text and the page address to our server, which passes them to an AI provider to extract the recipe. You review the result before anything is saved.

We send whatever the page shows you, so avoid pages behind a login or showing information meant only for you. A daily limit is counted against an anonymous per-install identifier; our server keeps only a counter. Each request — page text, address, prompts, and response — goes to an observability provider for debugging and quality, retained under its terms.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, names, emails, phone numbers, or location.
  • No advertising identifiers and no cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not train our own models on your recipes, notes, or the pages you read into the app.

Retention

On-device data lasts until you delete the app. Share links persist until removed on request. Analytics are aggregated without personal identifiers. Request traces and server logs follow each provider's default retention.

Third parties

We use a hosting provider (website, API, share-link storage), an AI provider (recipe extraction), an observability provider (request traces), analytics providers, and Apple (distribution, tips, device sync — see Apple's privacy policy).

We describe them by role rather than by name so this stays accurate when providers change. What each receives is described above and will not change without an update here.

Your rights and contact

There is no account or profile to access or delete. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device; contact us to remove a share link. If you live under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws, contact us to exercise your rights. BrewSmart is a general-audience app and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Material changes update the date above. Questions: brewsmart@ken0nek.com.
