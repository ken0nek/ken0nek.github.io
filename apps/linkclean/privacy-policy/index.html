LinkClean

Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 18, 2026

LinkClean ("LinkClean", "we", "us") removes tracking parameters from links before you share them. We built it to be private by default: there is no account to create, we run no server that sees your links, and the links you clean are processed on your device. This policy explains what the app and its website, linkclean.app, do, and what the app sends off your device.

What we collect

No account, and never your full links. LinkClean has no user accounts and asks for no name, email, or contact details. The links you clean are processed on your device, and we never receive the full link. Apart from the web requests described below, the only data about you or your links that the app sends off your device on its own is usage analytics, described next. As part of that, the app notes which website a cleaned link points to, so we can understand which sites people use LinkClean on, along with summary information about the tracking parameters it found. It never includes the full link.

Analytics

To understand which features people use and where the app can improve, LinkClean sends usage analytics, such as that a link was cleaned or that a screen was opened, to analytics providers. These providers may process and store this data outside your own country.

This data is tied to a random identifier for each install of the app, which does not contain your name or contact details. Beyond counts and basic interactions, what it takes from a link is limited to which website the link points to, plus summary information about the tracking parameters found in it, such as how many there were and what kind. It may also include a country or region.

Analytics never includes your search terms, the full link, its path, or any parameter values. A link cleaned from your clipboard is reported like any other cleaned link. This version of LinkClean does not include an in-app setting to turn analytics off.

What stays on your device

  • Your history. Each link you clean can be saved to a history list on your device. It is shared with LinkClean's extensions on your device, never leaves it, and is not synced anywhere. Turning off "Save history" stops new entries from being saved; clearing history, or deleting the app, removes them.
  • Your settings and custom parameters. Your preferences and any custom tracking parameters you add are stored on your device.
  • Clipboard. When LinkClean reads a link from your clipboard, automatically with auto-paste or when you ask it to, that link is treated like any other cleaned link, including in analytics. The rest of your clipboard stays on your device. You can turn auto-paste off in Settings.

When LinkClean reaches the internet

Cleaning happens on your device. LinkClean still makes ordinary web requests: to the websites your links point to, so it can show you a preview; if you turn on short-link expansion, to the services a short link passes through, and possibly the site it leads to, to find where it leads; to our own site, for information that keeps the app current; and to Apple, for App Store information. These run alongside the usage analytics described above. None of these sends your links to a server of ours, and none of them carries an account, your name, or your contact details.

Like any web request, each reveals your device's IP address and standard connection information to the service that answers it, under that service's own privacy policy. A request to a website carries only the link it is about, and never your history or your settings.

Things you choose to send

Beyond what is described above, LinkClean sends nothing on its own. When you share a link or email us, what you send goes only where you choose.

Our website

This policy also covers linkclean.app. The website uses analytics to count visits and to see which pages, links, and buttons people use. This data may include the address of the page you visited and the standard information your browser sends with any request. Like any website, it also sees your IP address, and analytics providers may process this data outside your own country.

What we do not collect or do

  • No account, name, email, or phone number.
  • No location data, other than a country or region that analytics may include.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The full links you clean are never sent to us or included in analytics. Analytics notes only which website a cleaned link points to and summary information about the tracking parameters found in it.

Children's privacy

LinkClean is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. It asks no one, children included, for a name, email, or other contact details.

Your rights

LinkClean has no user accounts and stores no name, email, or contact information. The links and data you create live on your device, and deleting the app removes them. The usage analytics we receive are tied to a random identifier for each install, and we hold nothing that connects it to your name or contact details. If you reside in a region with privacy laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, you have the rights those laws provide; you are welcome to contact us with any question or request.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
