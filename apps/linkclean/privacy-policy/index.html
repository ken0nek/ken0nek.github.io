LinkClean

Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 9, 2026

LinkClean ("LinkClean", "we", "us") removes tracking parameters from links before you share them. We built it to be private by default: there is no account to create, we run no server of our own, and the links you clean are processed on your device. This policy explains what the app does — and the little it sends off your device.

What we collect

No personal information, and never your full links. LinkClean has no user accounts and asks for no name, email, or contact details. The links you clean are processed on your device, and we never receive the full link. The only data the app sends off your device is anonymous, aggregate usage analytics, described next. As part of that, the app includes the website domain of a cleaned link (for example, youtube.com) so we can understand which sites people use LinkClean on — never the rest of the link, and never anything that identifies you.

Analytics

To understand which features people use and where the app can improve, LinkClean sends anonymous, aggregate signals — for example, that a link was cleaned or that a screen was opened — to a privacy-focused analytics provider designed not to identify you personally.

These signals are tied only to a random, per-install identifier that is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID. The one thing derived from a link that they include is its website domain (for example, youtube.com) — never the full link, its path, its query string, or any parameters or values. They never include your clipboard, your search terms, or any text you enter — only counts, basic interactions, and that domain. This version of LinkClean does not include an in-app setting to turn analytics off.

What stays on your device

  • Your history. Each link you clean can be saved to a history list kept in private storage on your device. It is shared between LinkClean and its share extensions on your device, but it never leaves your device and is not synced to iCloud or anywhere else. Turning off "Save history" stops new entries from being saved; clearing history — or deleting the app — removes them.
  • Your settings and custom parameters. Your preferences (such as auto-paste and save-history) and any custom tracking parameters you add are stored locally on your device.
  • Clipboard, only when you enable auto-paste. If you turn on auto-paste, LinkClean reads a link from your clipboard when you open or return to the app, so it can offer to clean it. This happens on your device; your clipboard contents are not sent anywhere.

Link previews — when LinkClean reaches the internet

To show a title or thumbnail for a link — in your history, or when you use Copy as Markdown — LinkClean fetches it directly from your device to the website the link points to, not through any server of ours. We never see it.

This is the same kind of request your browser makes when you open a link, and it may reveal your device's IP address and similar standard request information to that website, under that website's own privacy policy. LinkClean requests only the already-cleaned link, so tracking parameters are not sent.

What we do not collect or do

  • No account, name, email, or phone number.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt — there is nothing to track.
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The full links you clean are never sent to us or included in analytics — only a cleaned link's website domain (for example, youtube.com) is included, as anonymous analytics described above.

Children's privacy

LinkClean is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. It has no accounts and collects no personal information, so it collects none from children. The anonymous usage analytics described above contain no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

LinkClean has no user accounts and stores no name, email, or contact information, so there is no profile for us to access, correct, or delete — we hold nothing that identifies you. The links and data you create live on your device, and deleting the app removes them. The anonymous analytics we receive are aggregate and not linked to you, so they cannot be traced back to an individual to retrieve or erase. If you reside in a region with privacy laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, you have the rights those laws provide; you are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes
