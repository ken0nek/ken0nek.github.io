TicTacGone

Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 19, 2026

TicTacGone ("TicTacGone", "we", "us") is tic-tac-toe in which each player keeps at most three marks, and everything happens on your device. It has no accounts, makes no network requests, and we run no server. We collect no data about you, and nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

What TicTacGone stores

TicTacGone remembers your preferences, such as the theme and whether motion, sound and haptics are on. They are kept on your device and in your own device backups, if you use them. The game itself, including the computer opponent, runs on your device and is not saved or sent anywhere.

Device sensors

While you play, TicTacGone reads your device's motion sensors so the board's effects can follow how you hold it. The readings are used on the spot, never stored and never sent anywhere. TicTacGone asks for no access to your photos, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Analytics and tracking

TicTacGone contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps, no crash reporting, and no third-party services that receive data from the app.

Apple services

Apple distributes TicTacGone through the App Store and may give us aggregate figures, such as the number of downloads, that do not identify you. If you start a game with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple handles that request under its own privacy terms.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete.

Children's privacy

TicTacGone collects no information from anyone, including children, and asks no one for a name, email, or contact details.

Your rights

There is no account or profile for us to access, correct, or delete. If you live under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws, you have the rights those laws provide; contact us with any question.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
