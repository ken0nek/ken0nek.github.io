Nalavie

Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 4, 2026

Nalavie ("Nalavie", "we", "us") helps you look back on your family's photos, and the work happens on your device. It has no accounts and we run no server that holds your data. Your photos, face data, and your family's names and birthdates never reach us or any third party.

What Nalavie uses

Nalavie reads your photo library and the information stored with your photos, and keeps what you enter in the app, such as names and birthdates. Photos are analyzed on your device. Access to your library is read-only: the app never changes or deletes your photos. What Nalavie stores stays on your device and in your own device backups, if you use them.

Analytics

Nalavie uses a third-party analytics service to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It receives anonymous usage events, general measurements of how the app performs on your device, and a random identifier for your installation, not linked to you. These include purchase activity. Apple handles payments, so the service never receives your payment details. We use no advertising, no tracking across apps, no crash reporting, and no session recording. The service may process this information in countries other than your own.

Apple services

Nalavie may use Apple's on-device and cloud services that are designed to keep data private. Only text written on your device may be processed this way, never photos or face data, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Network requests

Nalavie may fetch small configuration files from our site and App Store information from Apple. These requests carry no family data. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP address to the service that answers, under that service's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete all of Nalavie's face data in the app's settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device; your photo library is not affected. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete.

Children's privacy

Nalavie is for parents. Photos of children, and their names and birthdates, are never sent to us or to a third party.

Your rights

There is no account or profile for us to access, correct, or delete. If you live under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws, you have the rights those laws provide; contact us with any question.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
