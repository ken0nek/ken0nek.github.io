Rondo

Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 25, 2026

Rondo ("Rondo", "we", "us") animates the counting numbers — 1, 2, 3, … — as factorization diagrams, nested rings of colored dots that dance from one number to the next. It is built to respect your privacy: there is no account, we ask for no personal information, and the app runs on your device. To understand how Rondo is used and to guide what we improve, the app may send anonymous, aggregate usage statistics that are not tied to your identity. This policy explains what little leaves your device, and when.

What we collect

No personal information. Rondo has no user accounts and asks for no name, email, or contact details, and it shows you no ads. We do not know who you are.

Anonymous usage statistics. To see which features are used and to decide what to build next, Rondo may use a privacy-focused analytics service to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data — for example, that a feature was opened or a diagram was shared, along with general technical details such as the app version and the kind of device. This data is designed not to identify you: it is tied only to a random identifier that is not linked to your name, your Apple ID, or any advertising profile, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites. We see broad patterns and counts, not individual people.

What stays on your device

  • Your favorites. Numbers you save to Favorites are kept in private storage on your device.
  • Your settings. Your display and playback preferences are stored on your device. So that the app, its widgets, and the Apple Watch app can stay in sync, they are shared across your own Apple devices using Apple's on-device frameworks — not through any account or server we operate.
  • Diagrams you share. When you share a diagram, Rondo hands an image to the iOS share sheet so you can send it wherever you choose. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app or service you pick.

Deleting the app removes everything Rondo has stored.

When Rondo reaches the internet

Two things send data off your device. The first is the anonymous usage statistics described above, which go to a third-party analytics provider — we run no servers of our own. The second is when you tap to learn more about a number, or open one of the credit links: Rondo opens that page in an in-app browser (Apple's Safari view), and the request goes directly from your device to that website (Wolfram Alpha for a number's reference page, or the sites we credit). Apart from these, Rondo is fully offline and makes no background requests.

Because you opened it, the number you are viewing is part of the address that is opened (for example, a Wolfram Alpha page for 184). As with any web page, the site you open may see your device's IP address and similar standard request information, under its own privacy policy. Rondo cannot read what happens inside that browser.

What we do not collect or do

  • No account, name, email, or phone number.
  • No personal profile — the usage statistics are anonymous and aggregate, not records about you as an individual.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing of it with data brokers or for advertising.
  • No purchase is required to use Rondo. Any in-app purchases — such as an optional tip jar to support development — are handled by Apple; we don't receive or store your payment information.

Children's privacy

Rondo is a general-audience app suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. It has no accounts and collects no personal information from anyone — the anonymous, aggregate usage statistics are not tied to any individual — so it collects no personal information from children.

Your rights

Rondo has no user accounts and stores no name, email, or contact information, so there is no personal profile for us to access, correct, or delete — and because the usage statistics are anonymous and aggregate, we cannot trace them back to any one person. The favorites and settings you create live on your device, and deleting the app removes them. If you reside in a region with privacy laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, you have the rights those laws provide; you are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
